美股参与度看板

口径与数据说明

定义：标普500成分股中，收盘价严格大于其自身 20 日 / 50 日简单移动平均线（均线含当日收盘价）的股票占比。分母只计历史足够长（满 20 / 50 根 K 线）且当天有收盘价的成分股，每天略有不同，点按曲线可看到当天的“上方只数 / 分母”。

价格口径：Yahoo Finance 原始收盘价（只复权拆股、不复权分红），与 Bloomberg / TradingView 的价格口径一致；分红复权的读数会系统性偏高，不采用。

样本：成分股名单取自 Wikipedia 当日名单（503 只）往回计算，存在幸存者偏差，历史读数仅供参考。每天的逐只明细（收盘价、SMA20、SMA50、是否在上方）保存在数据仓库的 data/details/。

交叉核对（2026-11-20 快照）：本页 20 日 18.92%（95/502）、50 日 42.71%（214/501）；Bloomberg 口径（博主引用）18.9% / 42.5%；2ndOcean（分红复权口径）19.4% / 42.9%；StockCharts（分红复权）约 21% / 43%。各家差异在 1–3 只股票的计数口径之内。

CNN 恐慌贪婪指数：0–100 分，分区为 0–24 极度恐慌、25–44 恐慌、45–55 中性、56–75 贪婪、76–100 极度贪婪。数据来自 CNN 官方 dataviz 端点；取不到时改用 GitHub 数据集 whit3rabbit/fear-greed-data（由同一 CNN 端点每日重建）。缺 2026-03-12 一天（两个来源都从 3/11 跳到 3/13，源头漏数），未插值，图上该处断线。

叠加对比图：SPY、QQQ、.VIX 为 Yahoo Finance 原始收盘价，与参与度同一交易日时间轴；.VIX 在休市日（2026-05-25、2026-09-07）的读数不在时间轴上，未纳入。因各线数量级不同，图中一律归一化后叠加：“起点=100”按相对涨跌比幅度，“区间归一化”按当前可见区间最低–最高缩放、只比形状与拐点；点按读数显示的仍是原始数值。

顶部数字极值变色：只给参与度、CNN 指数、.VIX 的最新数字变色（SPY / QQQ 不变色），分档如下——参与度：低于 20% 为红色（洗盘区）、20–30% 为橙色（偏低）、30–80% 为正常色、高于 80% 为绿色（极高）；CNN：0–24 红色、25–44 橙色、45–55 正常色、56–75 绿色、76–100 深绿色；.VIX：高于 35 红色、20–35 橙色、15–20 正常色、低于 15 绿色。

更新：每个交易日美东 19:30 自动取数（此时 CNN 当天最终值已定格）。取不到的数据不插值、不补数，写进 data/latest.json 的缺失说明。全部数据见 数据仓库，CSV 可直接用 Excel 打开。

Methodology & data notes

Definition: share of S&P 500 members whose close is strictly above their own 20-day / 50-day simple moving average (including the day's close). The denominator counts only members with enough history (20 / 50 bars) and a close that day, so it varies slightly; tap a chart to see that day's count / denominator.

Price basis: Yahoo Finance raw close (split-adjusted, not dividend-adjusted), the same basis as Bloomberg / TradingView. Dividend-adjusted readings run systematically higher and are not used.

Sample: the day's Wikipedia membership list (503 tickers) applied backwards, so history carries survivorship bias and is indicative only. Per-stock detail for each day (close, SMA20, SMA50, above or not) is kept in the repository under data/details/.

Cross-check (snapshot of 2026-11-20): this page 18.92% (95/502) and 42.71% (214/501); Bloomberg basis (as quoted) 18.9% / 42.5%; 2ndOcean (dividend-adjusted) 19.4% / 42.9%; StockCharts (dividend-adjusted) ~21% / 43%. Differences are within 1–3 stocks.

CNN Fear & Greed: 0–100; zones 0–24 extreme fear, 25–44 fear, 45–55 neutral, 56–75 greed, 76–100 extreme greed. Source: CNN's dataviz endpoint, falling back to GitHub whit3rabbit/fear-greed-data (rebuilt daily from the same endpoint). 2026-03-12 is missing at source; not interpolated, the line breaks there.

Overlay chart: SPY, QQQ and .VIX are Yahoo raw closes on the same trading-day axis as participation; .VIX values on market holidays (2026-05-25, 2026-09-07) are excluded. Lines are normalized before overlaying: "Start = 100" compares the size of moves; "Range-normalized" scales each line to its min–max in the visible window to compare shape and turning points. Readouts show raw values.

Colour of the top figures: only participation, CNN and .VIX are coloured (SPY / QQQ are not). Participation: below 20% red, 20–30% orange, 30–80% normal, above 80% green. CNN: 0–24 red, 25–44 orange, 45–55 normal, 56–75 green, 76–100 deep green. .VIX: above 35 red, 20–35 orange, 15–20 normal, below 15 green.

Updates: automatically at 19:30 ET each trading day (after CNN's daily value is final). Missing data is never filled in; gaps are listed in data/latest.json. All data is in the repository; the CSVs open directly in Excel.
