口径与数据说明

参与度定义

标普 500 成分股中，收盘价严格高于其自身 20 日 / 50 日简单移动平均线（含当日收盘）的股票占比。分母只计历史足够长的票（20 日线需满 20 根 K 线，50 日线需满 50 根）。成分股为 2026-11-20 当日 Wikipedia 名单（503 只）往回计算，存在幸存者偏差，历史读数仅供参考。

价格口径

Yahoo Finance 原始收盘价（只复权拆股、不复权分红），与 Bloomberg / TradingView 同口径。全部卡片与图表使用同一口径。

覆盖与缺失

  • 参与度：自 2024-11-22 起每个交易日一条，页面显示最近两年。分母 = 当天有收盘价且历史够长的成分股数，每天不同，点按曲线可看到当天的“上方只数/分母”。上市不足 20 / 50 天的票不计入对应分母；当天 Yahoo 没有收盘价的票不计入（每天的逐只明细保存在 data/details/）。
  • CNN 恐慌贪婪：缺 2026-03-12（CNN 官方与 GitHub 数据集均从 3/11 跳到 3/13，源头漏数），未插值，图上该处断线。来源：CNN 官方端点；取不到时用 GitHub whit3rabbit/fear-greed-data（每日从同一端点重建）。
  • .VIX：Yahoo 序列在 2026-05-25（阵亡将士纪念日）与 2026-09-07（劳动节）休市日仍有值，已排除，以 SPY 交易日为时间轴。
  • 所有曲线按日期对齐；缺失日期该线断开，不补数。

Methodology & data notes

Participation

Share of S&P 500 members whose close is strictly above their own 20-day / 50-day simple moving average (including the day's close). Only stocks with enough history count in the denominator (20 bars for the 20-day, 50 for the 50-day). Membership is the 2026-11-20 Wikipedia list (503 tickers) applied backwards, so history carries survivorship bias and is indicative only.

Price basis

Yahoo Finance raw close (split-adjusted, not dividend-adjusted), the same basis as Bloomberg / TradingView. Every card and chart uses this one basis.

Coverage & gaps

  • Participation: one row per trading day since 2024-11-22; the page shows the latest two years. Denominator = members with a close that day and enough history, so it varies; tap the chart to see that day's count/denominator. Stocks listed under 20 / 50 days are excluded from that denominator; stocks with no Yahoo close that day are excluded (per-stock detail is saved in data/details/).
  • CNN Fear & Greed: 2026-03-12 is missing at source (both CNN's endpoint and the GitHub dataset jump from 3/11 to 3/13); not interpolated, the line breaks there. Source: CNN's endpoint, falling back to GitHub whit3rabbit/fear-greed-data (rebuilt daily from the same endpoint).
  • .VIX: Yahoo has values on market holidays 2026-05-25 (Memorial Day) and 2026-09-07 (Labor Day); excluded, SPY trading days are the time axis.
  • All lines align by date; a missing date breaks the line, nothing is filled in.

Overlay chart

"Start = 100": each line is based on the first trading day of the visible window; the axis shows % change from that start. "Range-normalized": each line is scaled to 0–100 by its min–max in the visible window, to compare shape and turning points only. Readouts show raw values.
