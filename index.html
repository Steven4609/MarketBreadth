市场宽度和情绪报告

口径与数据说明

参与度定义

  • 收盘价严格高于自身 20 / 50 日均线（均线含当日）的成分股占比
  • 分母：满 20 / 50 根 K 线、且当天有收盘价的成分股
  • 点按曲线可看当天的“上方只数 / 分母”

价格口径

  • Yahoo Finance 原始收盘价（只复权拆股，不复权分红）
  • 与 Bloomberg / TradingView 同口径
  • 分红复权的读数会系统性偏高，不采用

样本

  • Wikipedia 当日名单（503 只）往回计算
  • 有幸存者偏差，历史读数仅供参考
  • 每天保存逐只股票的计算明细，可逐只核对

CNN 恐慌贪婪指数

  • 来源：CNN 官方端点；备用 GitHub whit3rabbit 数据集
  • 缺 2026-03-12：源头漏数，未插值，图上断线
  • 取每个交易日收盘后 CNN 显示的读数并锁定。CNN 事后会把历史点改成更晚时刻的读数（如 2026-11-20 收盘后为 9.67，媒体当日报道 9.7，CNN 历史后来显示 18.59），这类改动不采用

叠加对比图

  • 起点=100：比较涨跌幅度
  • 区间归一化：只比形状与拐点
  • 点按显示原始数值；.VIX 休市日（05-25、09-07）不计入

.VIX 波动率指数

  • 标普500 期权隐含的未来 30 天年化波动率
  • <15 平静 · 15–20 正常 · 20–30 升温 · 30–35 紧张 · >35 恐慌
  • Yahoo ^VIX 收盘价；休市日的读数不计入

AAII 散户情绪调查

  • 个人投资者对未来 6 个月股市的看法，每周四公布，图上画在公布日
  • 长期均值（1987 年起 2,050 周，本页自算）：看多 37.6%、看空 31.5%、多空差 +6.1
  • 只存近两年数据；数据版权归 AAII，仅供个人参考

自动更新

  • 每个交易日美东 19:30 自动取数
  • 缺数不插值、不补数，缺了哪项会如实记录
  • 已定稿的历史锁定；每天与官方数据核对，不一致时先通知、确认后才修改

Methodology & data notes

Participation

  • Share of members closing strictly above their own 20 / 50-day MA (incl. the day)
  • Denominator: members with 20 / 50 bars and a close that day
  • Tap a chart for that day's count / denominator

Price basis

  • Yahoo Finance raw close (split-adjusted, not dividend-adjusted)
  • Same basis as Bloomberg / TradingView
  • Dividend-adjusted readings run high; not used

Sample

  • Today's Wikipedia list (503 tickers) applied backwards
  • Survivorship bias: history is indicative only
  • Per-stock calculation detail is kept for each day for checking

CNN Fear & Greed

  • Source: CNN endpoint; fallback GitHub whit3rabbit dataset
  • 2026-03-12 missing at source; not interpolated, line breaks
  • Each trading day's post-close CNN reading is recorded and locked. CNN later overwrites history points with later readings (e.g. 2026-11-20 read 9.67 after the close and was reported as 9.7 that day, but CNN's history now shows 18.59); such changes are not adopted

Overlay chart

  • Start = 100: compares the size of moves
  • Range-normalized: compares shape and turning points only
  • Readouts show raw values; .VIX holidays (05-25, 09-07) excluded

.VIX volatility

  • 30-day implied volatility of S&P 500 options, annualized
  • <15 calm · 15–20 normal · 20–30 elevated · 30–35 stressed · >35 panic
  • Yahoo ^VIX close; holiday readings excluded

AAII sentiment survey

  • Individual investors' 6-month outlook, published Thursdays; plotted on the release date
  • Long-run averages (2,050 weeks since 1987, computed here): bullish 37.6%, bearish 31.5%, spread +6.1
  • Only the last two years are stored; data © AAII, for personal reference

Auto update

  • Each trading day at 19:30 ET
  • Gaps are never filled; any missing item is recorded as missing
  • Final history is locked; it is checked against official data daily, and any difference is reported and changed only after confirmation
